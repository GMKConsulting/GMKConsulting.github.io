About

My path to the boardroom was not a straight line. I started out as a pilot in training with the Canadian Armed Forces, and worked as a lifeguard, a muffler shop hand, a gas pipeline worker and a sales and insurance professional.

Public office taught me how to serve many stakeholders at once. Banking taught me discipline with other people’s money. Building a company taught me what it feels like when the plan meets reality.

I am based in Panama and open to remote board and advisory roles, with some travel.

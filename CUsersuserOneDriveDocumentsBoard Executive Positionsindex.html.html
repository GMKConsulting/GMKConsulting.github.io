A record of service and building

Banking and public service, then a company of my own. Each taught a different side of oversight.

  1. 1985–2000

    Sales and management positions, Bank of Montreal, Investors Group, Royal Bank and HSBC Bank

    Fifteen years across four financial institutions, building a grounding in sales, client relationships and management.

  2. 1990

    President, Penticton Chamber of Commerce

    Led the local business community’s voice to council and the public.

  3. 1990–1998

    Councillor, City of Penticton (three terms)

    Also sat on the Regional District, the hospital board and the library board, balancing budgets and service needs across public bodies.

  4. 2000–2016

    Senior Consultant, Regional Vice President and National Vice President Sales, Standard Life

    Helped develop and implement a business consultancy program guiding small business owners on marketing, client segmentation and operational efficiency. As National Vice President, oversaw a staff of 100 across Canada generating $4 billion in product sales. Collaborated with the Vice Presidents of Customer Service, Marketing, Legal, HR and Product Development to efficiently run the Sales line of business.

  5. 2019–2023

    Lead co-founder, Crossmedia Canada (Sensea Immersive)

    Brought the first permanent Immersive Experience to the Vancouver, Canada area, in a 33,000 sq ft facility designed and built from scratch. Led incorporation, lease agreements, facility construction, operating structure and all marketing, with HR and expense oversight in a COO role. Wound the company down in 2023 and moved the equipment to a partner in Mexico for better market opportunities.

  6. Now

    Virtual Reality Experience, Panama City

    Working with my partner at Crossmedia Mexico to open a facility in mid 2027 featuring VR experiences for all ages.

About

My path to the boardroom was not a straight line. I started out as a pilot in training with the Canadian Armed Forces, and worked as a lifeguard, a muffler shop hand, a gas pipeline worker and a retail department manager before banking.

Public office taught me how to serve many stakeholders at once. Banking taught me discipline with other people’s money. Building a company taught me what it feels like when the plan meets the lease, the contractors and the payroll.

I am based in Panama and open to remote board and advisory roles, with some travel.
